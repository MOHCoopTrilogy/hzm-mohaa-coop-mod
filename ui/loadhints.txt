[G] Sprinting? Tap CTRL to slide. Keep holding it to drop prone at the end.
[G] Run at a waist-high wall and jump to vault over it.
[G] Hold Shift to sprint. You can't fire until you stop and bring your gun back up.
[G] The white outline over your health bar is your stamina. It turns amber when nearly empty.
[G] Sprinting, jumping, vaulting, sliding and holding your breath all use the same stamina.
[G] Heavy guns tire you faster and slow your stride. Pistols are the lightest to carry.
[G] Hold right mouse to aim down the sights for much tighter shots.
[G] While aiming, hold Shift to hold your breath: a steadier aim and less kick.
