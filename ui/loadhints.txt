// ---- COOP: how it works
[C] 250 damage in one burst knocks you down - but if your health runs out first, you die.
[C] Downed, you bleed out in 90s (45s for a head wound), but you can still shoot your pistol.
[C] Downed? Tap F to call a medic, or hold F with a medkit to revive yourself in 5 seconds.
[C] Downed with no medkit? Don't hold F - holding it without a kit means giving up.
[C] Hold F beside a downed teammate for 5 seconds to revive them. It costs you one medkit.
[C] Health does not regenerate. Medkits and health packs are your only healing.
[C] You spawn with one medkit and can carry two. A medkit heals you to full.
[C] Stand still while using a medkit - moving or getting hit cancels it.
[C] Press F next to a wounded teammate to heal them to full with one of your medkits.
[C] Each extra squadmate gives every German a chance to spawn a twin.
[C] Rounds landing near a German wreck his aim for a moment and drive him into cover.
[C] Kill the Officer, then plant explosives on his radio (F) to stop reinforcements for good.

// ---- GENERAL: how your soldier works
[G] Stress comes from near misses, wounds, exhaustion and movement, and it widens your shots.
[G] Fully rattled, your bullet spread can double. Settle down before a long shot.
[G] Crouching, going prone or bracing your gun all take the edge off stress.
[G] Near-miss stress fades in seconds; stress from wounds stays until you heal.
[G] The grey tunnel vision as rounds crack past is suppression - it is costing you accuracy.
[G] Hold Shift and move forward to sprint - you can't fire until you stop and raise the gun.
[G] The white outline over your health bar is your stamina. It turns amber when nearly empty.
[G] Heavy guns tire you faster: a full bar is ~24s of sprint with a rifle, ~20s with an MG.
[G] Aim down the sights (right mouse) to cut your bullet spread almost in half.
[G] While aiming, hold Shift to hold your breath: semi-auto shots tighten and recoil drops.
[G] Moving scatters your shots. Stop before you fire.
[G] Your gun sets your pace: pistol 98%, SMG 94%, rifle 89%, BAR/MG 78%, shotgun/launcher 74%.
[G] Stop at a sill, crate or wall edge; when the bipod icon glows, press F to brace your gun.
[G] Below 30% health you limp: slower, and no sprinting, sliding or holding your breath.
[G] Mounted MGs overheat after about 2 seconds of non-stop fire. Shoot in bursts.
[G] Tap G to throw a grenade. Hold G to throw it farther - but holding also shortens the fuse.
