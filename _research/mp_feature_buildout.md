## Verdict

Most of the mod survives into standard MP, and the reason it does not today is smaller and dumber than it looks: 78% of the documented feature set is either already gametype-agnostic or gated on one of about nine shared hooks, while the things that actively break MP are mostly undocumented framework behaviours - a dmflags stomp, an allies-only team force, a scoreboard-text clobber - written when "not single-player" and "co-op" were the same thing. The single most valuable measurement I made this pass is that a universal script entry point already exists and the mod already owns it: 69 of the 71 real stock MP map scripts exec global/ambient.scr (the only exceptions are mp_bizertefort_obj.scr and mp_ship_lib.scr), which turns "wire 61 features" into "boot one script and re-key one flag". The 61 needs-wiring rows collapse onto nine hooks, and three of them - a per-map MP boot, a dm_team-agnostic coop_isActive, and engine-owned g_statefile - carry 34 features between them. Against that, the honest bad news is in three places: the whole MP-facing progression lane (XP, challenges, medals, weapon unlocks) has no earning source because every counter is bumped from an AI death and rejects a PvP kill at challenges.scr:1460; DBNO cannot ship to any round-based mode until Player::IsCoopDbno() is bridged into DM_Team::IsDead (dm_manager.cpp:555) or a wiped team can never be declared dead; and standard MP without a dedicated server is not standard MP, while bug-330 says our game.dll segfaults loading obj/obj_team1 under one and has not been re-tested since 2026-07-07. The plan's ten stages are the right spine and I am extending it, not replacing it - my Phase 0 through 10 all name the stage they sit on. Two things I will not pretend to know: whether bug-330 still reproduces (Stage 3 exists to answer that, and everything in Phase 6 onward is downstream of the answer), and whether the 23 mod arenas survive the AI handlers turning on for the first time. And one decision only the user can make: player-on-player gore was removed deliberately by bug-792, so T1-T3 gore in MP is a product call, not an engineering one.

## Order of work

Nine steps. Each says what the user can actually play at the end of it. Existing plan stages are named where a step is one of them.

STEP A - STAGES 1, 2, 3 + PHASE 0. "MP is startable and honest." (small; 2-3 sessions)
  Stage 1 pk3/cfg pass: delete ui/coop_weaponselect_suppress.urc, uncap lobbytest.cfg's sv_maxclients, split the coop-only forces out of autoexec.cfg, delete `set developer 1` (:44), restore playermodel 0 on the options button, restore sv_team_spawn_interval. Stage 2: restore the PvP damage clause. Stage 3: run the two-boot bug-330 diagnostic and clamp maxentities. Phase 0: the Level::Init cvar-restore block.
  Do the Tier-3 security items HERE, not later - they are all trivial and they are the difference between "we can test this on a box" and "we cannot".
  PLAYABLE AFTER: standard MP works, on our binaries, for the first time. Two people can shoot each other on obj/obj_team1. The 44 works-now rows are all live: composure, slide, hit flinch, damage roll, ADS iron sights, weapon lag, holstered weapons, the full audio mixer, reverb, suppression FX, gun smoke, post-FX, shadows, MG42 overheat on ~63 stock emplacements, the whole Service Record menu. That is the free win, and it is most of the feel.
  BLOCKING UNKNOWN: whether bug-330 still reproduces. If it does, everything from Step D onward is behind fixing it, so this step MUST end with an answer logged to buglog.

STEP B - STAGE 4 + PHASE 1. "The framework boots on MP maps." (medium; 2-3 sessions)
  The discriminator, the deferred inCoopMode rework in aihandler/itemhandler, and coop_mod/mp_main.scr launched from global/ambient.scr (69/71 coverage, measured) plus the two stragglers.   Gate the entire Tier-1 and Tier-2 cut list in this same commit. Once mp_main.scr runs, every one of those framework behaviours is reachable on an MP map, so the gates and the boot are ONE change, not two.
  PLAYABLE AFTER: same as Step A, but nothing regresses when our scripts start running, and the tooling (regression harness with an MP roster, the MP map-rotation variant) can now smoke-test 70 stock MP maps for parse and precache breakage - which is worth more than any single feature, because an MP boot has fewer visible symptoms than a co-op one.

STEP C - PHASE 2. "Teams are real." (medium; 2 sessions)

  PLAYABLE AFTER: THIS IS THE BIG ONE. Sprint, prone, ADS on its own button, bash, gun bracing, emotes, limp and the movement model all work AND LOOK RIGHT in standard MP, and `g_modernmp 0` genuinely gives classic MOHAA. Prone stops being an invisible accuracy exploit. This is the first step where the user can hand someone a server and say "this is the mod".

STEP E - PHASE 4. "Kills mean something." (medium; 2-3 sessions)

  PLAYABLE AFTER: co-op-flavoured PvE modes on MP-shaped maps. Good, but this is a new product surface, not the delivery of the existing one.

TOTAL: roughly 27-37 working sessions, of which Steps A-F (about 15-19) deliver the thing the user actually asked for - standard MP working fully, with the mechanics on by default and one switch to get classic MOHAA.

TWO PRODUCT DECISIONS ONLY THE USER CAN MAKE, and neither is an engineering question.
  1. PLAYER GORE IN MP. T1-T3 were excluded from players deliberately by the bug-792 verdict ("blood appearing on my skin from being shot"). In MP the payoff is gore on OTHER players - but the honest payoff is small: Player::Respawn clears the tier latch and props (player.cpp:3100-3116) and DeadBody copies model, angles and eFlags but never s.surfaces (:12042-12055), so a disfigured MP head is visible only between death and respawn and the corpse left behind is always clean. T4 UV wounds CAN be made "everyone but you" cleanly and cheaply; T1-T3 cannot. Do not ship T1-T3 to MP without asking.
  2. WHICH MODERN MECHANICS ARE DECLARED RULESET CHANGES. Spawn protection duration, DBNO in FFA, whether AI kills score in a PvE arena, the ADS zoom clamp value, whether limp stays on in FFA (below 30% health you move at 60% speed AND cannot sprint, which is a death spiral no AI ever exploited). Each is defensible; none is defensible SILENTLY. A public server running g_modernmp 1 is a MOD RULESET, not stock MOHAA, and the host needs a list.
