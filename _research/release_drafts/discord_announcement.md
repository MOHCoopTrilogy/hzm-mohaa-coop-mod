**@everyone  HZM Coop — v1.2.0 is coming, and it's a big one. 🩸**

We've been heads-down and this update is stacked. Highlights:

**🩸 New gore & blood system**
Bullets leave wounds where they actually land — real bullet holes on uniforms *and* exposed skin at
the true impact point. Soldiers get bloodier as they take hits, killing blows throw extra spray, and
the dead slowly pool blood beneath them (yes, officers and reinforcements too). Grounded and dark,
not cartoon-red. You stay clean — this is for the enemy.

**🔫 The Armory**
A full loadout picker — 69 weapons across 6 classes, live 3D preview, stat/caliber/recoil readouts,
one-per-class rules, and padlocks with unlock requirements on gear you haven't earned. Cosmetics too:
cycle **skins** and **helmets** right there. Opens from the main menu (even offline) and the lobby —
and your picks now apply **the instant you spawn**. No more spawning with the wrong gun.

**✅ Ready-Up on briefings**
Squad roster + ready checkmarks + "Press F to Ready Up" right on the mission briefing. Everyone ready →
5-second countdown → go. No more waiting around.

**🔊 Bleed-out audio**
Subtle wet death-rattle SFX near fresh corpses. Small touch, big atmosphere.

**🧱 Build-mode cover**
New Cover Objects category — wrecked tanks, trucks, cars, boulders, concrete — solid, and the cover
system can actually use them. (Plus a fix so big placed objects are truly solid now.)

**🖥️ Quality of life**
- Modern resolution picker w/ ultrawide + up to 4K, plus an easy Borderless / Fullscreen / Windowed
  selector
- **Your options actually save now** across restarts
- MP taunts finally play (both sides were silent!)
- Enemy grenades actually explode, wounded Germans aren't bullet sponges anymore, random spawn-time
  "death" grunts gone, tank turret visible when you man it, and a pile more fixes

Full notes drop with the release. **Update via the in-game updater**, and please use **Report a Bug**
in-game to send us anything weird. 🫡
