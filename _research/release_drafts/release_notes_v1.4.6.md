**Six systems that were built but never actually running, plus a co-op scaling fix.**

**Voice commands work now — press X.** The radio wheel has been silent on every campaign map in all three games. Two separate causes: the voice lines were tagged deathmatch-only, and the key that opens the wheel was already taken by the rifle bash.

**Hit markers.** White X when you land a hit, red when they go down.

**Squad radar was lying to you.** It reached about one room, and anyone you couldn't see was drawn stuck to the rim regardless of real distance — direction only, never distance. Both fixed; range now covers real ground.

**Difficulty stopped punishing you for being down a man.** Enemy numbers, accuracy and reinforcement wave size counted *connected* players, so a spectator or a teammate bleeding out still counted as a full fighter. It also picked enemy counts in the level's first frame while people were still loading, so a four-player game often populated as if two of you turned up.

**The revive prompt stopped vanishing.** An objective banner popping mid-revive wiped the prompt and both progress bars for the rest of the 90-second bleed-out.

**Objective reward drops.** Smoke or binoculars near your objective, on nine maps.

**Also:** weapon bracing on cover and while prone, enemies shot in the hand draw a sidearm instead of standing there empty-handed, and an Omaha Beach pass.

None of this has been playtested yet — if something misbehaves, **Report a Bug** in the menu comes straight to us.
