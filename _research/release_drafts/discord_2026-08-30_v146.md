## Text as posted

**MOH Trilogy Coop — what's landing in the next build 🫡**

Big one for anyone playing with a full squad.

**📻 Voice commands actually work now**
The radio wheel has been silent on every campaign map since forever — in all three games. Two
separate reasons, and both had to be fixed: the voice lines were tagged deathmatch-only, *and* the
key that opens the wheel was already taken by the rifle bash. **It's on X now** — press X, pick a
group, pick a message.

**🎯 Hit markers**
White X when you land a hit, red when they go down.

**📡 Your squad radar was lying to you**
It reached about one room, and anyone you couldn't see was drawn stuck to the rim no matter how
far off they were — direction only, never distance. Fixed, and the range now covers real ground.

**⚖️ Difficulty stopped punishing you for being down a man**
Enemy numbers, accuracy and wave size counted *connected* players — so a spectator or a teammate
bleeding out still counted as a full fighter. It also picked enemy counts in the level's first
frame, while people were still loading, so a four-player game often populated as if two of you
turned up. Both fixed.

**🩹 The revive prompt stopped vanishing**
An objective banner popping mid-revive wiped the prompt and both progress bars for the rest of the
90-second bleed-out, with no way to get them back.

**🎁 Objective reward drops**
Smoke or binoculars near your objective — off since July because of a bug that was actually fixed
in the same commit that disabled it. Nine maps have drop points now.

**🔫 Also**
Weapon bracing on cover and while prone, an enemy shot in the hand now draws a sidearm instead of
standing there empty-handed, and Omaha Beach got a pass — less of a coin-flip on the run in, a lot
more going on around you.

**🚧 Straight with you:** all of the above loads and runs clean, but none of it has been playtested
yet. If something in here misbehaves, **Report a Bug** in the menu comes straight to us — that's
exactly what we need right now.
