# v1.7.1 - Roll Out

More ways to fight, and now with armor. This release adds a new hide-and-seek mode, a full
vehicle system, brings two modes onto the single-player campaign maps, and squares away a
couple of multiplayer rules.

## New: Prop Hunt
- One team hides, disguised as ordinary map objects; the other team hunts them down after a
  short head start. Props press USE to change shape, and can move but can't shoot back. Last
  props standing (or the clock) decide the round.

## New: Vehicles and emplacements
- Drive tanks and jeeps - one player drives, a second mans the gun.
- Man fixed Flak 88s and Nebelwerfers.
- Anti-tank pickups: Allies get the bazooka, Axis the panzerschreck (never each other's).
  Tanks shrug off small arms - only a rocket kills one, so those pickups matter.
- Destroyed vehicles blow up and respawn; hold USE beside an unoccupied enemy vehicle to
  capture it. A crew HUD shows the vehicle, its health, and your crew.
- Available on five converted single-player campaign maps.

## New: Campaign maps for Base Assault and Push
- Base Assault and Push now play on real single-player campaign maps, not just the deathmatch
  arenas - reached from a "Campaign Maps" button on the team setup screen.

## New: Spawn protection
- A few seconds of invulnerability right after you respawn, dropped the instant you open fire,
  so you can't be farmed on the spawn.

## Modes tuned
- Demolition is now truly two-way: either team plants a charge on the enemy's bomb site.
- Freeze Tag gains a meltgun - aim and fire at a frozen teammate to weld them free faster than
  standing next to them.

## Fixes
- Medkits no longer let you channel a heal at full health, and can no longer refill a
  Down-But-Not-Out absorption pool (an accidental near-invincibility). They top you to your
  real max health, nothing more.

Coop players: nothing here changes your game. Every multiplayer system is gated off on coop
maps and the build enforces that isolation automatically.
