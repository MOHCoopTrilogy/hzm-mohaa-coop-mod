## Option A — full post

**@everyone  HZM Coop — patch notes 🫡**

Mostly a fix pass, and one of them has been hunting us for a while.

**🚚 The Normandy truck ride is quiet again**
If you've played m1l1 lately you'll have had Germans opening up on you *during* the scripted
ride into the checkpoint — sometimes seconds after the map loaded. That's fixed, and the cause
turned out to be a good one: the engine command that marks you "don't shoot this one" was
declared **twice**, and for players it resolved to a *cheat toggle* that ignores its argument and
flips the flag instead of setting it. So every attempt to protect you switched it back off again.
It looked random because the result depended on how many times the code happened to run.

It's fixed at the root, which means it fixes the same thing on **every scripted ride in the
trilogy** — Normandy, the Bastogne intro, the e1l3 tank ride, and eleven more — not just the one
map where we noticed it.

**🪖 Your squad stays in the truck**
The allies riding with you were standing up mid-ride and playing a dive animation. They stay put.

**🎒 One kit, not three**
You were being handed your loadout three times per spawn (two of them silently). Now once — and
your Armory picks still apply the moment you spawn.

**🔧 Also**
- Removed the fidgety bolt-cycling idle animation — you asked for that one back on the 21st, and
  it turned out it was still running on your machine even though we'd switched it off.
- Turret gunners get their weapon back on dismount instead of it staying invisible.
- The low-ammo weapon tilt is gone. It was under one unit of movement; nobody could see it.

**🚧 Still working on**
Wall cover isn't ready — leaning and blind-firing out of a doorway still pick the wrong side.
We know exactly why now and it's being fixed properly rather than patched again.

Keep the reports coming — **Report a Bug** in the menu goes straight to us.
