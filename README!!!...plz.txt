Readme:

O.K. this is a mod/skin pack fills with skins that show America's Awsomeness. This is filled with about 5 different skins that you can put into your game. They are:

Armdude's Airborne Elite
Armdude's Big Red 1
={DTA}=Bootson's Avatars
={DTA}=Bootson's Amderican Compass
={DTA}=Bootson's Axis/Allies Winner Skins

This includes all of these skins in one big .pk3 file or each on individually if you like only one of them.


.:INSTALLATION:.

Just put the .pk3 of the file you would like in your game in this folder:

C:\Program Files\EA Games\MOHAA\Main

.:UN-INSTALLATION:.

Just remove the .pk3 from the same folder that you put it in to.


And jsut to clarify what the "={DTA}=Bootson's Axis/Allies Winner Skins" are. they are just the screen when it sais "Axis Win!" and "Allies Win!". it's just a couple cool pics of those parts!
